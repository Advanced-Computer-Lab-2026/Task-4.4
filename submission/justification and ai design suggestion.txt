justifications

1-descriptive error messages: “Please review your entries” didn’t tell users what was wrong. The form now points out each problem and explains how to fix it. 

2-Easier and less confusing editing: Clicking the three dots on someone else’s post only showed an unhelpful message. I removed that menu and added a visible Edit button to the user’s own posts, making editing easier to find.

3-Clearer search behavior: Results already updated automatically, so Apply was confusing. I removed it and added a short explanation and a live result count. Users can still clear all filters with one click.

AI-assisted design refinement

I refined the idea of “more descriptive errors” into guidance users could act on: explain the problem beside the field, show a summary, and take them to the first mistake.
